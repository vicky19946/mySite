About Vicky Hou

Connecting Content Vision with Business Objectives.

"Ensuring brand consistency while optimizing for performance across luxury touchpoints."

I am Vicky Hou, a Content Strategist with over 7 years of deep-domain experience in content strategy, integrated campaigns, and digital and Social CRM communication, specializing in prestige luxury and premium beauty brands.

Throughout my career, I have successfully connected content strategy with overarching business objectives, ensuring brand consistency while optimizing for performance. My experience working with premier global houses such as Hermès, Dior, Estée Lauder, De Beers, Montblanc, and Guerlain has equipped me with the skills to manage end-to-end content planning—aligning key messages with conversion goals and driving measurable performance improvements.

I hold a proven track record in cross-functional collaboration, coordinating seamlessly across creative, media, and e-commerce teams to enhance project execution efficiency and elevate content quality.

My data-aware mindset enables continuous refinement of content priorities and strategies by leveraging actionable analytics. Furthermore, I am adept at guiding agency partners to elevate output quality, maintaining absolute fidelity to the brand's heritage.

In this role, my objective is to ensure that brand messaging remains consistent and impactful across all channels, while bolstering the linkage between content output and measurable business success.
